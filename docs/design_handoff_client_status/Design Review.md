# Design director review — Northfold monitoring product

Reviewed: Overview, Websites, website drawer, Incidents, Reports (history + review), Monthly Report PDF. Client management and a website detail page did not exist at review time.

Severity: **S1** breaks trust or a core flow · **S2** clear usability or consistency cost · **S3** polish.

## What's working (evidence)

- **Identity:** condensed Archivo with monospace timestamps, the check-window strip, hatched "no data" and the dark status bar. It reads as one product and doesn't depend on the accent colour.
- **Honesty:** stale results are labelled "Stale" with their last-known value. Inconclusive results are drawn as ◌ and excluded from counts. Domain expiry shows "n/a" with the reason instead of a guessed date. The report says "first found 28 Sep" rather than implying a whole month.
- **Restraint:** no gradients, no KPI-card grid, square corners. Shadows appear only on overlays. Red is kept for down and critical.
- **App and report:** the report shares the app's type and semantic colours, while agency branding is limited to the cover, rules and section numbers.
